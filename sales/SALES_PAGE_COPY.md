## FAQ

### Is this a licensed bank?

No. It is software source code. The buyer must provide licensing and regulated partners.

### Can I connect my own payment provider?

Yes. OpenBank NG is designed around provider adapters so buyers can connect approved providers.

### Is it specific to Nigeria?

Yes. It is oriented around Nigerian banking assumptions, NGN/kobo money handling, Nigerian bank codes, and BVN/NIN-ready KYC workflows.

### Can agencies use it for client projects?

Yes, subject to the final license terms selected for the commercial package.

### Can it go live immediately?

No buyer should go live without legal review, provider integration, production security audit, and compliance approval.
